Jonathan J. Ferrucci

Co-Founder & Chief Operating Officer

Overview

Jonathan J. Ferrucci is a serial entrepreneur and strategic operator with over 20 years of experience building and scaling ventures across emergency infrastructure, energy, and financial technology. He co-founded AERI in 2015, an emergency response and infrastructure company that grew to 450+ employees, securing major contracts with FEMA, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, and state agencies for wildfire prevention, emergency restoration, and disaster recovery services.

Active in cryptocurrency markets since 2017, Jonathan has executed over $1 billion in trading volume and specialized in AI-driven trading systems for three years. He owns significant oil and gas assets currently being converted into AI data centers, positioning BHAI Holdings at the intersection of traditional energy infrastructure and next-generation AI compute power.
